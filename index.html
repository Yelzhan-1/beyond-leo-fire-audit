Why ISS rules do not carry over

Gravity feeds the flame

Without buoyancy, shutting off fans is the first ISS response. NASA Glenn finds that is unreliable on the Moon and Mars.

Airflow rule on the Moon

Smoke and dust overlap

Station detectors missed some plastics; lunar chamber tests show regolith can mimic smoke in the rover cabin.

False-alarm rule in the rover

Small cabins change tools

A CO₂ extinguisher in a small pressurized rover can exceed safe limits.
