Assumptions

Beyond LEO Fire Audit

Ten ISS-era fire-safety rules of thumb, checked against lunar and Martian cabins with NASA microgravity combustion data. Built for the 2026 NASA Space Apps Challenge, Flame in Freefall, by Team NZE, Almaty.
