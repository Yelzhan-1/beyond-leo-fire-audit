About

An audit of ISS fire rules, built by Team NZE

Beyond LEO Fire Audit is an entry to the 2026 NASA Space Apps Challenge by Team NZE from Almaty. It asks one question: which fire-safety rules that work on the ISS still hold on the Moon and Mars?

The challenge

The project responds to Flame in Freefall, one of the 2026 NASA Space Apps Challenge challenges. It uses the resources listed on the challenge page. See where each one appears.

The team

Team NZE, Almaty

Author: Zhanibek.

The code, the data records and the page text are in the open: source repository. Found a mistake in a record or a source? Open an issue there, and name the rule and cabin.

How AI was used

  • Research, drafting and code. An AI model (Claude by Anthropic) helped to read the cited documents, draft the evidence records and the page text, and write the code.
  • Checking. Every record names its source and the place in it, so a reader can check it. Automated tests protect the verdict engine from accidental change.
  • Verdicts. They are computed by fixed rules, not by a language model. Where SI fits explains the one optional SI feature, which is off unless a service is configured.

What this is not

This is an independent project that uses public NASA and partner-agency material. It is not a NASA product and carries no endorsement. The verdicts are screening estimates for discussion, not a safety certification of any vehicle. Read the limits.
